LensLine Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026 · 中文

LensLine is a teleprompter for recording yourself. It doesn't collect, store or share any personal data on our side. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Scripts and settings are stored only on your device.
  • Camera: the front camera shows you behind the script, and records video only when you turn recording on and start a take.
  • Microphone: in Follow mode, LensLine listens while you read so the script can scroll with your voice. It also records sound with your takes.
  • Photos: takes are added to your photo library only when you choose to save them. LensLine can't read your library.

Speech recognition

Follow mode turns your voice into text with Apple's speech recognition. It runs on your iPhone whenever your language supports it. Otherwise, the audio is processed by Apple's speech service under Apple's Privacy Policy. LensLine never sends audio or text to its own servers, and has none.

Deleting your data

Delete a script in the app, or delete the app to remove all of its data. Takes you saved to Photos stay there until you delete them in Photos.

Children

LensLine doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: lenslinedev@gmail.com.

LensLine 隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 11 月 8 日

LensLine 是一款录制自己时使用的提词器。我们不会收集、存储或分享任何个人数据。没有账号、广告、数据分析或追踪。

保存在你 iPhone 上的内容

  • 脚本和设置只保存在你的设备上。
  • 相机：前置摄像头的画面显示在脚本后面；只有当你打开录制并开始一条录制时才会录像。
  • 麦克风：在跟读模式下，LensLine 会在你朗读时聆听，让脚本跟着你的声音滚动；录制时也会录下声音。
  • 照片：只有在你选择保存时，录制的视频才会添加到你的照片图库。LensLine 无法读取你的图库。

语音识别

跟读模式使用 Apple 的语音识别把你的声音转换成文字。只要你的语言支持，识别都在 iPhone 上完成；否则音频会由 Apple 的语音服务处理，适用 Apple 隐私政策。LensLine 从不把音频或文字发送到自己的服务器，也没有任何服务器。

删除你的数据

在 App 中删除脚本，或删除 App 即可移除它的所有数据。已保存到“照片”的视频会保留，直到你在“照片”中删除。

儿童

LensLine 不会有意收集任何人（包括儿童）的信息。

政策变更与联系方式

如果本政策有变更，新版本会发布在此页面并更新日期。如有问题，请联系：lenslinedev@gmail.com。
